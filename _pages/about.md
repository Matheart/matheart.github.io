Hi! I am a second-year PhD student at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by [Surbhi Goel](https://www.surbhigoel.com/) (Computer Science) and [Enric Boix-Adsera](https://eboix.github.io/) (Wharton Statistics & Data Science). I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science & Mathematics from HKUST in 2025. My research is supported by the AWS Asset Fellowship at Penn.


My research interests lie in the **theory and empirical science of deep learning and LLMs**: running experiments that probe how these models work, then building theory to explain what those experiments reveal. Recently, I have been focusing on the following questions:

- **Scaling Science.** How can we pre-train, post-train and scale models in a principled way, and how can we understand their training dynamics, both in theory and in practice?
- **Alignment Science.** How can we keep a model aligned as its capabilities improve? I am interested in misalignment phenomena that remain poorly understood, and in safety monitoring.
